# Code of Conduct

We expect everyone participating in Uslu Medya's GitHub spaces to help create a respectful and professional working environment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with courtesy regardless of background, identity, or experience.
- Give specific, constructive feedback. Critique the work and explain your reasoning.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, and privacy.
- Disclose mistakes and help correct them.
- Keep discussion relevant to the work and make room for different perspectives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, sexual misconduct, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information are unacceptable. Spam, impersonation, and repeated disruption of a discussion are also unacceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other associated project discussions. Other Uslu Medya repositories may adopt this code explicitly or provide their own.

## Reporting a concern

Email [usluyapimorg@gmail.com](mailto:usluyapimorg@gmail.com) with the subject **Code of conduct concern**. Include links, relevant context, and your preferred contact method. Share only the personal information needed to understand the concern.

Avoid posting sensitive reports in public issues. Reports will be handled as privately as practicable, with information shared only as needed to assess and address the situation.

## Handling reports

The account owner or a designated maintainer assesses the report and decides on a proportionate response. Anyone involved in a report should step aside from assessing it where another uninvolved reviewer is available. If a report concerns the person who receives it, request an uninvolved reviewer and share only what you are comfortable disclosing.

Responses may include a request to change behavior, removal of content, a warning, temporary participation restrictions, or removal of repository access. The severity, context, and repeated nature of the conduct inform the response.

If you believe a decision was based on incomplete information, reply privately with the additional context and request a review.
